Technical Case

Diagnosing a blood and plasma warming system

One of the most technically significant maintenance cases I worked on involved diagnosing faults on a Barkey PlasmaTherm blood and plasma warming system. My role covered diagnosis, inspection, disassembly and reassembly, and final verification. Component soldering was performed by a senior engineer.

Two faults, two root causes

The first fault involved the system's cover-detection mechanism. Investigation traced the problem to a missing reed sensor on the PCB. The sensor itself was soldered onto the PCB by a senior engineer; my contribution was identifying the fault, carrying out the relevant inspection and PCB removal/re-attachment, and verifying the repair.

A separate overtemperature fault, identified as Error 092, was traced to insufficient water level in the system. The low water level exposed the heating rods, resulting in the observed error. After the corrective work, the equipment was verified through calibration and functional testing in both User and Service modes.

Maintenance Workflow

From reported fault to verified repair

Corrective Maintenance Process

01

Fault Replication

Reproduce the reported behaviour and identify the conditions under which the fault occurs.

02

Inspection & Diagnosis

Use visual inspection, measurements, circuit understanding, and equipment documentation to narrow down the cause.

03

Corrective Action

Carry out the appropriate repair, component replacement, adjustment, or reassembly under supervision.

04

Verification

Confirm correct operation through functional testing, calibration, and required safety checks.
